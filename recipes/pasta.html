Lasagna Recipe

This is Pasta dish filled with all sorts of meats and cheeses, it goes really delicious once cooked in oven.

It's an italian dish, comes with herbs and all sorts of spices inside.

The ingriedients:

  • Cheese(loads of it)
  • Meats(Pork+Beef)
  • Herbs
  • Pasta

Step of how to make:

  1. Pre-heat the oven
  2. Start layering the pasta
  3. Add beef and pork
  4. Cheese it up
